Handling a twin-screw boat

  • Opposing the engines (one ahead, one astern) pivots the boat and, thanks to prop walk, also shifts it sideways — it is not a passive drift, expect to actively manage the rotation.
  • The rudder is modeled but never required. Left off-center, it drags and adds unwanted yaw the moment you use an engine or thruster — check it before you blame the throttle.
  • Both thrusters in the same direction move you sideways with little rotation; that is what they are for.
  • Prop walk is not symmetric between the two engines — the same maneuver can feel different depending on which side you are working against.

About the model

Real Bayliner 4788 dimensions, mass, and estimated shaft spacing; drag, added-mass, prop-walk, and thruster coefficients are gameplay-tuned, not sea-trial calibrated. No waves, shallow-water effects, bank suction, or mooring lines.
